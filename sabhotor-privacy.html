Privacy Policy for SABHOTOR Moments Bot

SABHOTOR Moments Bot is a local desktop tool by Sourabh Pawar. It finds moments in a gameplay video, cuts them into vertical Shorts, and, only when you choose to post, uploads them to your own YouTube channel.

What the app does

The app runs on your own Windows PC. It reads a video file you choose, analyses its audio and video locally, creates short clips on your PC, and, only if you run it with the post option, uploads the clips you choose to YouTube.

Data we collect

The app does not run a server and does not collect analytics. The only Google data it uses is what is needed to upload to your YouTube channel, with your consent:

  • Your Google account email and YouTube channel information, used only to sign in and upload.
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token, stored locally on your PC in a file called token.json, next to the app.
  • The video files you choose to process, which stay on your PC except for clips you choose to upload to YouTube.

How your data is used

Your Google sign-in and YouTube channel data are used only to upload videos to your own channel when you ask the app to post. The app does not share, sell, or send your data to any other person, company, or server.

How your data is stored

Your sign-in token is stored only on your PC, in token.json. Your client secret is stored only on your PC, in client_secret.json. You can delete token.json at any time to sign out. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google Account, under Security, Third-party access.

Google API services

The app uses the YouTube Data API. Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third parties

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party trackers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact sabhotorgaming@gmail.com.
